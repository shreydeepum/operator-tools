How the numbers work (plain-English assumptions)
1. Unbranded AI shopping prompts Public 2026 holiday retail forecasts put AI-influenced e-commerce near ~20%, with over 60% of shopping prompts naming no brand upfront. The model treats 65% of bot-assisted shoppers as up for grabs.
2. How bot readiness changes your share Stores with messy pricing/stock ("Blind") capture ~25% of bot-driven buyers. "Half-Ready" stores capture ~65%. "Bot-Ready" stores capture 115% (stealing unbranded gift shoppers from slower rivals).
3. Shrinking click pool = pricier ad auctions As shoppers move from search links to AI answers, the remaining ad clicks get more competitive (modeled as +0.35% effective ad-cost squeeze per 1% AI shift, reduced when you shift spend to owned lists & retail media).
4. The panic-discount trap Extra price cuts come straight out of profit kept per order. The break-even volume jump uses exact margin math: discount / (margin - discount). At 40% margin, a 20% price cut forces +100% more orders just to break even.
